# AI Literacy for CS Faculty

A ~30-minute AI literacy talk for the Fall 2026 CS faculty meeting, built with reveal.js. Four parts, outside in: what it is (AI → ML → generative → LLMs, a click-through timeline 2012–2026, the word "GPT", the next-token engine, how a 2026 model is trained), the curve (METR time horizons, cost, context, the jagged frontier, open vs. commercial models, what's hot in October 2026, how to read an AI headline), agents (one annotated agent loop, a student's laptop, the harness in one picture, MCP, agent teams, four failure modes, prompt injection and the rule of two), and the classroom (the lost apprenticeship rung, whether models can do our assignments, what randomized trials say about learning, the five-level ladder, what 763 CS educators changed, MIT's August 2026 report, a two-lane plan for Monday). It builds on the AI Committee's *AI Literacy Tutorial* draft.

Play online: https://aquastar.github.io/ai-literacy-faculty/

Keys: Space or arrows to advance, S for speaker notes (with timing cues), Esc for overview.
